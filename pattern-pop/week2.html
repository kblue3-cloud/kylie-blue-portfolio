Final revision / Week 3

Process note

01 / Feedback + testing

I focused on the professor’s request for a playful surprise after the final level. Testing also showed that new players benefited from a clearer first action and a stronger signal that the six levels were building toward one idea.

02 / Changes

I kept the Week 2 six-level progression, added an “observe, select, reconsider” guide, refined the pacing and response copy, and created a discoverable hidden ending. The reward connects the final payoff directly to hallucination: the player’s attention turns visual noise into an imagined system.

03 / AI + my decisions

I used ChatGPT/Codex to audit the full interaction path, implement accessible HTML/CSS/JavaScript, and test desktop and mobile behavior. I chose the concept, puzzle rules, visual language, six-level progression, pacing, wording, and hidden-reward direction.
